I'm Goodness, a social media and content strategist who brings structure to your ideas without losing what makes your voice yours.

My work brings together content, communication, and coordination. Across virtual assistance, marketing support, and independent social media management, I've helped turn ideas into plans, plans into posts, and posts into conversations.

That work has taken me from skincare content for Luméra Skin to LinkedIn personal branding for a B2B SaaS founder, alongside supporting international clients with their content and audience engagement. Different brands, different voices, each needing a strategy that feels like them.

For Luméra Skin, that meant growing Instagram views from 18.7K to 82.7K in 90 days. For a supported LinkedIn post, it meant reaching over 3.8 million impressions. But the work isn't only about numbers. It's also about helping you show up with clarity, consistency, and something meaningful to say.
